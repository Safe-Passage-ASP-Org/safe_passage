# User Data Deletion Instructions for Safe Passage

Safe Passage respects your privacy and gives you full control over your personal data. In accordance with Meta and platform policies, here is how you can delete your data associated with Safe Passage:

### Option 1: Automatic Deletion via Facebook Settings
1. Log in to your Facebook account.
2. Go to **Settings & Privacy** > **Settings**.
3. Under the left navigation, select **Apps and Websites**.
4. Find **Safe Passage** and click **Remove**.
5. When removing the app, check the option to request that Safe Passage delete any data obtained through Facebook.

### Option 2: Request Deletion via Safe Passage Admin
To permanently purge your account, profile details, and registered mobile devices from the Safe Passage system:
1. Send an email request from your registered email address to `robmalik19@gmail.com` with the subject: **"Request Account Data Deletion"**.
2. Include your username and registered email.
3. Your account, authentication credentials, MFA session history, and device keys will be permanently purged within 48 hours.
