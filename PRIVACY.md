# Privacy Policy for Safe Passage

**Effective Date:** November 16, 2026  
**Application:** Safe Passage  

### 1. Information We Collect
Safe Passage collects only the necessary information required for identity verification and multi-factor authentication:
* Basic account profile information (Full Name, Username, Email Address) provided directly by you or via authorized OAuth identity providers (such as Facebook, GitHub, or GitLab).
* Registered device information (Device Name, OS type, and cryptographic public keys) used strictly for out-of-band push authentication and biometric verification.

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use your information exclusively to:
* Authenticate your identity and verify sign-in requests.
* Deliver multi-factor authentication (MFA) approval prompts to your registered mobile devices.
* Secure administrative and user accounts against unauthorized access.

### 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure
Safe Passage does not sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal data with third parties or advertisers. Information is only processed internally to complete authentication workflows.

### 4. Data Security & Storage
All authentication tokens and sensitive credentials are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols and stored securely. Passwords and sensitive keys are hashed using strong cryptographic standards (PBKDF2/SHA-256).

### 5. User Data Deletion & Inquiries
You may request the deletion of your account and associated authentication data at any time by contacting your Safe Passage system administrator or by following the instructions in our [Data Deletion Policy](DATA_DELETION.md).
